# Default-package class — research areas and output sections

Use this file when the filing requests entry of default or default judgment. The filing is typically a *bundle* of documents rather than a single paper, and the bundle's required contents are usually specified by a procedural rule.

## Research areas (in addition to common.md)

- Request for entry of default: required form contents, signature, and any mailing-to-defendant requirement
- Default-judgment package contents enumerated by the governing rule
- Case summary or factual-basis declaration supporting the judgment amount
- Interest calculation (computation of prejudgment interest, where applicable)
- Cost memorandum or analogous costs claim
- Military-status declaration (compliance with the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act)
- Proof of service or proof of mailing of the default-request paperwork to the defaulting defendant
- Proposed judgment
- Disposition of remaining unserved or fictitious defendants (where the jurisdiction permits)
- Path: clerk default vs. court default; declaration prove-up vs. evidentiary hearing
- Pre-default service requirements (e.g., statement of damages in some categories of personal-injury actions)

## Output sections (in addition to common.md)

- Request for Entry of Default
- Required Documents in the Default-Judgment Package
- Case Summary / Factual-Basis Declaration
- Interest Calculation (conditional)
- Cost Memorandum
- Military-Status Declaration
- Proof of Mailing
- Proposed Judgment
- Disposition of Unserved / Fictitious Defendants (conditional)
- Statement of Damages (conditional)

These lists are research hints, not output templates. Sub-categories and items emerge from the rules retrieved for the specific default situation in the specific jurisdiction.
