# Motion class — research areas and output sections

Use this file when the filing class is a motion or noticed application.

## Research areas (in addition to common.md)

- Notice of motion content (opening paragraph, party-to-party identification, basis and relief)
- Motion-specific substantive requirements — the rule governing this particular motion type. Query the corpus for it; do not assume the general motion rules cover the substantive requirements specific to this motion's category.
- Memorandum of points and authorities (required content, page limits, tables of contents and authorities)
- Supporting declarations
- Exhibits (index, separation/bookmarking, deposition exhibits, foreign-language translation)
- Request for judicial notice (separate-document requirement, copy to court and parties)
- Evidence at hearing (form — declaration vs. oral testimony — and procedure for seeking oral testimony)
- Tentative ruling and hearing procedure (posting, notice of intent to appear, courtesy copies)
- Proposed order (lodging vs. attaching, electronic format, post-ruling proposed-order procedure)
- Meet-and-confer requirements (where the specific motion type requires them)

## Output sections (in addition to common.md)

- Constituent Documents
- Notice of Motion
- Motion-Specific Substantive Requirements
- Memorandum of Points and Authorities
- Memorandum Page Limits
- Declarations
- Exhibits
- Request for Judicial Notice (conditional)
- Evidence at Hearing
- Proposed Order
- Tentative Ruling / Hearing Procedure (conditional)

These lists are research hints, not output templates. Sub-categories and items in the actual checklist emerge from the rules retrieved for the specific filing in the specific jurisdiction.

## Concept grep bank (motion-specific)

Run these against **every** in-scope document with `grep_rules`, in addition to the common bank in `common.md`. Floor, not ceiling — add terms for the specific motion type (e.g. for a cost motion, `cost|tax|memorandum of costs`).

- Notice & motion papers: `notice of motion|grounds|relief|moving papers`
- Memorandum: `memorand|points and authorities|page limit|exceed|reply|closing|table of (contents|authorities)|summary of argument`
- Evidence & support: `declaration|affidavit|exhibit|deposition|lodg|translat|request for judicial notice`
- Hearing & ruling: `tentative|oral (argument|testimony)|appear|continu`
- Order & conference: `proposed order|\[?proposed\]? order|meet.?and.?confer|conf(er|erence)`
